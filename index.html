Good to know

When are you open?
Tuesday to Friday 11:30 am to 8:30 pm, Saturday 10 am to 8:30 pm and Sunday 10 am to 6 pm. We're closed on Mondays.
Do you take reservations?
Walk-ins are always welcome. For a party of 8 or more, call (661) 260-3502 to reserve.
Can I get takeout or delivery?
Yes. Call ahead to order for carryout, or order delivery through the delivery apps.
Do you serve breakfast?
On weekends, Saturday and Sunday from 10 am to noon, with plates like beef tapa, tocino and longanisa, each served with white rice and egg.
Is there a lunch special?
Tuesday to Friday, 11:30 am to 3 pm: lunch plates from 16.00 with steamed rice and stir-fried vegetables or egg rolls.
Do you have vegetarian dishes?
Yes. Veggie egg rolls, crispy fried tofu, stir-fried veggies and bok choy are vegetarian, and the eggplant omelette is gluten free.
Where do I park?
We're on the first floor of the Valencia Entertainment Center at 23460 Cinema Dr, Suite D, with a private lot and street parking.
